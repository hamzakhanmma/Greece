Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Spain work experience

Where will students be travelling to in Greece?

Students will be travelling to a selected location in Greece as part of the international work experience programme. The exact accommodation and work placements will be organised by the college and partner organisations before departure.

Do I need a passport to travel?

Yes. All students must have a valid passport before travelling. It is recommended that your passport remains valid for the duration of the trip and for several months after returning to the UK. Students should also keep photocopies or digital copies of their passport in case of emergencies.

Will students be supervised during the trip?

Yes. College staff and programme coordinators will provide supervision and support throughout the trip. Students are still expected to behave responsibly, follow safety guidance, and respect local laws and rules during their stay.

What type of work experience will students do?

Students will take part in structured work experience placements related to their course where possible. This allows them to gain international experience, develop professional skills, and learn about working in another country.

What should I pack for the trip?

Students should pack comfortable clothing suitable for warm weather, appropriate clothing for work placements, personal hygiene items, travel documents, and any required medications. It is also recommended to bring travel adapters and basic first aid items.

Is travel insurance provided?

The programme may include travel insurance, but students should confirm this with their college coordinator before travelling. Insurance usually covers medical emergencies and travel disruptions but may not cover high-risk activities such as extreme sports.

Are quad bikes safe to use in Greece?

Quad bikes are considered high-risk vehicles and are involved in many tourist accidents in Greece. Students are strongly advised to avoid using quad bikes unless they are experienced and properly insured. Always follow local driving laws and safety guidance.

What should I do in an emergency?

In an emergency, students should contact local emergency services immediately and inform the supervising staff member. The main emergency number in Greece is 112, which can be used for police, ambulance, and fire services.

What are the rules regarding alcohol and drugs?

Students must follow all local laws in Greece. Illegal drugs are strictly prohibited and can result in serious legal consequences. Alcohol should only be consumed responsibly and in accordance with local regulations and programme rules.

Who should I contact if I have questions before the trip?

Students should contact their course tutor or the programme coordinator at Leicester College for any questions regarding travel arrangements, documents, accommodation, or work placements.
